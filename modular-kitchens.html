Modern Modular Kitchens in Gorakhpur

L-Shape, U-Shape, Parallel & Island kitchens designed for Indian cooking habits.

Ergonomic Kitchens Crafted For Every Home

The kitchen is the heart of your home. We engineer modular kitchens that maximize counter space, optimize the cooking golden triangle (hob, sink, refrigerator), and withstand heavy spices and moisture.

Choose from 100% boiling-water-proof (BWP) ply, anti-scratch acrylic shutters, PU matte paint, seamless quartz stone tops, and German hydraulic hinges with lifetime rust guarantees.

Kitchen Features & Inclusions

  • Tandem Box Drawers: Heavy-duty weight capacity up to 45kg per drawer.
  • Quartz Waterfall Countertops: Stain-resistant, non-porous, and scratch-proof.
  • Smart Corner Units: Magic corner carousels and tall pantry pull-outs.
  • Built-In Appliance Ready: Seamlessly houses chimneys, hobs, and microwave ovens.
