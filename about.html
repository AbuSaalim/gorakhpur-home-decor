Our Story & Heritage

Born in Gorakhpur, Engineered for Long-Lasting Beauty

Founded over 8 years ago with a mission to bring world-class interior finishes to eastern Uttar Pradesh, Gorakhpur Home Decor has evolved into Gorakhpur's most recommended decor and modular interior studio.

We recognized that homes in Gorakhpur and surrounding Purvanchal districts face distinct climate challenges — severe monsoon humidity, damp wall seepage that flakes off wall putty within months, and termite issues in conventional woodwork.

By introducing 100% waterproof fluted PVC panels, heavy washable vinyl wallpapers, and marine-grade BWP modular kitchens & wardrobes, we solved these problems permanently. Today, operating from our showroom at Taramandal, we have successfully transformed more than 500 living spaces.
